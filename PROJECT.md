# Visaworx OS

**Owner:** Klar Travels  
**Status:** Build  
**Primary route:** `/visaworx`

## Product

Visaworx is the visa intelligence and human consultation vertical inside the existing Klar Travels website.

It is not a standalone website, CRM, analytics platform, customer portal, payment system, or generic visa-processing product.

## Position

Visaworx owns:

- Visa Intelligence
- Visa Readiness
- Risk Reduction
- Expert Decision Support
- Human Guidance
- Reliable Support

> Expert guidance. Stronger preparation. Confident visa decisions.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Integrate with the existing Klar Travels ecosystem.
- No authentication.
- No CRM.
- No analytics module.
- No customer dashboard.
- No visa-approval guarantees.
- AI assists; human experts decide.
- Never invent visa rules, timelines, requirements, or success probabilities.
- Use the approved Visaworx logo exactly as supplied.
- Use the original Klar Travels logo exactly as supplied.
- Never recreate, redraw, restyle, approximate, or substitute either logo.
- Use authentic, human-first imagery and restrained content density.
- Everything reusable lives under `common/`.
- A shared artifact must exist once and be reused everywhere.
