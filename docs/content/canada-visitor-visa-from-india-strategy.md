## 4. Verified Facts and Citation Ledger

| Fact | Value | Official source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visitor visa fee | CAD 100 per person | IRCC fee list | 2026-08-21 |
| Visitor visa family maximum | CAD 500, family of 5 or more applying at same time and place | IRCC fee list | 2026-08-21 |
| Biometrics fee | CAD 85 per person | IRCC fee list | 2026-08-21 |
| Biometrics family maximum | CAD 170, family of 2 or more eligible applicants together | IRCC fee list | 2026-08-21 |
| Biometrics validity | 10 years; no re-collection needed within that period for a further visitor visa, work or study permit | IRCC biometrics | 2026-08-21 |
| eTA route conditions | held a Canadian visitor visa in past 10 years **or** hold a valid US non-immigrant visa | IRCC eTA eligibility | 2026-08-21 |
| eTA mode restriction | air travel only; visitor visa still required by car, bus, train or boat including cruise ship | IRCC eTA eligibility | 2026-08-21 |
| eTA nationality precondition | open to citizens of **certain** visa-required countries only | IRCC eTA eligibility | 2026-08-21 |

### The one hedged claim, and why

**India's absence from the eTA-eligible list is stated as "not currently among them", with an
explicit instruction to confirm on the official page.**

The IRCC eligibility page returns 403 to automated fetches, so it could not be read directly.
Two independent searches restricted to `canada.ca` failed to surface India on that list, and
the 2023 expansion announcements do not include it. That is good evidence but it is not the
same as reading the list, and the list has been extended more than once — Indonesia and
Malaysia were added in 2026.

So the guide states the position as current-and-checkable rather than permanent, and tells the
reader to verify. If India is ever added, this guide inverts, like the UK ETA guide.

### Deliberate omissions

- No processing times. IRCC publishes dynamic estimates that change constantly.
- No claim about approval likelihood or which visa office is faster.

## 5. AEO / GEO

**Direct answer target:** *Do Indian citizens need a Canadian visitor visa, or can they use an eTA?*

**AI search summary (about sixty words):**

> Indian citizens generally need a Canadian Temporary Resident Visa. The eTA alternative is
> open only to citizens of certain visa-required countries, covers air travel only, and
> requires either a Canadian visitor visa held in the past 10 years or a valid US non-immigrant
> visa. The visa costs CAD 100 plus CAD 85 biometrics, which stay valid for 10 years.

**Relationship made explicit:** eTA eligibility is a property of nationality *first* and
documents second. Holding a US visa does not create eligibility on its own.

## 7. Conversion Strategy

Unusually, the CTA has a concrete arithmetic payoff: checking biometrics validity and family
fee maximums before applying can save real money. That is the hook, and it is honest — it costs
the reader nothing to check and sometimes saves CAD 85 per person.
