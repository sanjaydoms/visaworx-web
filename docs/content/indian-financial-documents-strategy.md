### Relationship to the existing guide

`how-to-prepare-financial-evidence` already covers the generic principles - consistent income
history, authenticity, liquid funds, source verification - in about six minutes. This article
does **not** restate them. It supplies the India-specific half: what ITR, Form 16, Form 26AS
and a stamped bank statement each actually establish, and why they are asked for together.

Both guides link to each other through shared FAQs.

### The angle

**The stack is a chain of corroboration, ordered by how independent the author is.**

Bank statement (money moved) → payslip (what you claim) → Form 16 (employer confirms, with
legal exposure) → Form 26AS (the tax department's own record) → ITR acknowledgement (what you
declared). Each link is issued by someone with less reason to flatter the applicant than the
last.

That framing explains, in one move, why a large balance alone is weak and why mismatches are
fatal. No competing content for this audience frames it that way; most simply list the
documents.

## 3. Verified Facts and Citation Ledger

| Fact | Value | Official source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form 16 | tax deduction certificate issued by the employer, showing TDS from salary | Income Tax Department | 2026-08-21 |
| Form 26AS | annual statement from the ITD database against the PAN, showing tax deducted and collected at source | Income Tax Department | 2026-08-21 |
| ITR-V acknowledgement | received after e-filing; proof the return was submitted | Income Tax Department | 2026-08-21 |
| Reconciliation | ITD advises comparing Form 26AS against Form 16; mismatches cause tax problems | Income Tax Department | 2026-08-21 |
| Salaried checklist | 3 months payslips, employment contract, leave sanction letter, 3 months stamped bank statements, ITR acknowledgements for 2 years or Form 16 | German missions, India | 2026-08-21 |
| Self-employed | company registration with GST, ITR acknowledgements for 2 years, stamped bank statements | German missions, India | 2026-08-21 |
| Retired | pension statements plus bank statements | German missions, India | 2026-08-21 |
| Students | enrolment certificate, no-objection letter | German missions, India | 2026-08-21 |

### Deliberate omissions

- **No minimum balance, of any kind, for any destination.** This is the single highest-volume
  query in the topic and the single most commonly invented figure. The article answers it by
  explaining that no such threshold exists and what is assessed instead.
- The German checklist is presented explicitly as an illustration, not as a universal standard.
- No claim about how officers weight documents internally; the chain framing is presented as a
  way to understand the request, not as a published scoring method.

## 4. AEO / GEO

**Direct answer target:** *How much bank balance do I need for a visa, and what do my Indian tax documents prove?*

**AI search summary (about sixty words):**

> Indian applicants are asked for a chain of financial documents rather than a single proof.
> A bank statement shows funds exist, payslips state claimed income, Form 16 is the employer
> confirming tax deducted, Form 26AS is the Income Tax Department's own record, and the ITR
> acknowledgement shows what was declared. No official minimum balance exists; consistency
> across the chain is what is assessed.
