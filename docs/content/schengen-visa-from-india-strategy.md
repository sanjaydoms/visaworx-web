Every figure below is sourced. **Nothing in the article states a number that is not on this list.**

| Legal basis | EU Visa Code, Regulation 810/2009 | European Commission | 2026-08-21 |
| Visa fee, adult | EUR 90 | German Federal Foreign Office (India) | 2026-08-21 |
| Visa fee, child 6–11 | EUR 45 | German Federal Foreign Office (India) | 2026-08-21 |
| Decision period | 15 calendar days, extendable to 45 | German Federal Foreign Office (India) | 2026-08-21 |

| Latest advisable | at least 15 calendar days before travel | German Federal Foreign Office (India) | 2026-08-21 |
| Passport | issued within last 10 years, valid 3 months beyond return, 2 blank pages | German Federal Foreign Office (India) | 2026-08-21 |

| Fingerprint reuse | prints from a previous Schengen application reusable within 5 years | German Federal Foreign Office (India) | 2026-08-21 |
| EES progressive start | 12 October 2025 | European Commission | 2026-08-21 |
| EES full operation | 10 April 2026; replaces passport stamping | European Commission | 2026-08-21 |
| ETIAS status | not in operation; scheduled Q4 2026; **visa-exempt nationals only** | eu-LISA, travel-europe.europa.eu | 2026-08-21 |

### Deliberate accuracy caveats written into the article

- The India document list is what **German missions** publish. Other Schengen consulates
  publish their own lists. The article says so explicitly rather than presenting one
  consulate's checklist as universal.
- No minimum bank balance is stated. No Schengen-wide official figure exists, and inventing
  one is the most common error in competing content.
- No approval rates, no refusal statistics, no appointment-availability claims.
- The illustrative traveller is clearly signposted as hypothetical.

## 10. Open Questions

1. **Origin framing.** This is the first India-origin content on a site organised by
   destination. If India-origin becomes the house style, the ten country pages need a
   consistent position, and `countries.ts` currently treats India only as an unpublished
   *destination*.
2. **Tables.** `ContentBlock` has no table variant. Fee and timeline data is carried as lists
   instead. Adding a `table` block means extending the union and `GuideContentRenderer`.
3. **FAQ volume.** FAQs are global entities rendered on `/visaworx/resources/faqs`. Six were
   added here. At scale, per-article FAQs will need either a category filter on that page or a
   guide-scoped FAQ field.
