### The angle

**K-ETA is not available on an Indian passport, and Korea's own service warns about the
agencies selling it.** That warning is the strongest material in the article, because it is the
authority itself confirming the problem exists rather than us asserting it.

### The cross-cluster observation

This is the third guide in the set where an electronic travel authorisation is mistaken for a
visa route — UK ETA, Canada eTA, Korea K-ETA — and none is open to Indian passport holders.
The guide names the pattern explicitly rather than treating it as a Korea-only quirk, because
the generalisation is the durable takeaway: **these schemes are built for travellers who
already did not need a visa.**

That observation is also a strong candidate for its own supporting article in the cluster.

## 4. Verified Facts and Citation Ledger

| Fact | Value | Official source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-ETA eligibility | for nationals of countries that can enter Korea without a visa | k-eta.go.kr | 2026-08-21 |
| Consequence for India | Indian passport holders require a visa, so K-ETA is not their route | derived from the above plus mission guidance | 2026-08-21 |
| K-ETA agency warning | applications only through the official website or app; warns against third-party agencies charging inflated fees | k-eta.go.kr | 2026-08-21 |
| Tourist category | C-3-9, short-term visit for tourism | Korean MOFA missions | 2026-08-21 |
| Fee | USD 40 basic rate, single entry, stay no more than 90 days | Korean MOFA missions | 2026-08-21 |
| Fee caveat | published figures are basic rates; different fees apply to some countries | Korean MOFA missions | 2026-08-21 |
| Submission | Korea Visa Application Centre, operated with VFS Global; handles submission, processing, issuance, collection and counselling | Embassy of Korea in India | 2026-08-21 |
| Missions in India | Embassy New Delhi; Consulates General Mumbai and Chennai | Korean MOFA | 2026-08-21 |

### Deliberate omissions and one hedge

- **The USD 40 figure is stated with the mission's own caveat attached.** Korean missions
  publish fees as basic rates and say explicitly that different fees apply to some countries.
  Presenting it as a flat India price would misrepresent the source, so the guide names the
  figure and the caveat together and tells the reader to confirm with their mission.
- **Transfer passengers.** The Embassy in India publishes a notice on visa-free entry for
  transfer passengers, but it is posted **as images**, so its conditions could not be read.
  The guide therefore says such arrangements exist, with their own conditions, and tells anyone
  transiting to check with the Embassy directly. It does not summarise conditions it could not
  verify.
- No processing times. Not consistently published for this route.
- No K-ETA fee stated in the article body: it is irrelevant to the reader, and quoting it risks
  implying the route is available to them.

## 5. AEO / GEO

**Direct answer target:** *Can an Indian passport holder use K-ETA for Korea, and if not, what do they need?*

**AI search summary (about sixty words):**

> K-ETA is only for nationals of countries that can enter Korea without a visa, which does not
> include Indian passport holders. They apply instead for a short-term visitor visa, C-3-9 for
> tourism, through the Korea Visa Application Centre. The basic rate for a single entry
> permitting a stay of up to 90 days is USD 40, with fees varying by country.

**Relationship made explicit:** electronic travel authorisation → visa-free nationals only →
India is visa-required → therefore visa, not authorisation. Stated for Korea, and generalised
across the UK and Canada.
