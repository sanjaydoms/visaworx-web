### The angle

The differentiator is the **ETA exclusion**. GOV.UK confirms India is not on the ETA national
list, so Indian nationals must hold a Standard Visitor visa. Most competing content covers
"UK visitor visa documents" generically and does not address the confusion travellers actually
arrive with. This article leads with it.

Secondary differentiator: the **three-month application window** is narrower than Schengen's
six, which matters to anyone planning a combined UK-and-Europe trip and is rarely stated
side by side.

### Deliberate omissions

- **No processing time stated.** GOV.UK does not publish a standard visitor processing time on
  that page, and service-standard figures move. The article says appointment availability sits
  on top of processing rather than naming a number.
- **No minimum bank balance.** No published figure exists.
- No approval or refusal rates, no appointment-availability claims.
- The ETA national list is described as expanding, because it has been, and the article tells
  the reader to re-check it rather than treating today's exclusion as permanent.

## 7. GEO Optimization

**AI search summary (about sixty words):**

> Indian passport holders cannot use the UK Electronic Travel Authorisation, because India is
> not on the ETA national list. They need a Standard Visitor visa, which costs GBP 135 for up
> to six months, permits stays of up to six months, and can be applied for no earlier than
> three months before travel. Visaworx helps travellers assess preparation before applying.

**Relationship made explicit:** ETA → visa-exempt nationalities only → India not listed →
Standard Visitor visa required. This is the single fact the article exists to establish.
