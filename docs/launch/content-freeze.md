# Visaworx Content Freeze Policy

## 1. Content Freeze Protocol
Effective immediately prior to production launch, all structured data sources (`countries.ts`, `services.ts`, `guides.ts`, `faqs.ts`, `glossary.ts`) are under content freeze.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Brand Rules
1. Use approved Visaworx logo (`/brand/visaworx-logo.png`) and Klar Travels logo (`/brand/klar-travels-logo.png`) exactly as supplied.
2. Never recreate, recolor, filter, or distort logos.
3. Visaworx represents visa intelligence and human expert guidance. Never guarantee visa approval, 100% success, or embassy decisions.

## 3. Recorded exception to rule 2.2 (reversed logo artwork)

Both supplied logo files are dark ink on transparency and are illegible on the navy footer.
Until official reversed artwork exists, the footer serves placeholders derived from the
masters — ink recoloured to white, red accents and transparency unchanged:

- `public/brand/visaworx-logo-reversed.png`
- `public/brand/klar-travels-logo-reversed.png`

The masters are unmodified and still serve every light surface. This is a deliberate,
authorised exception, recorded here and in `common/artifacts/brand-rules.md`. Replace both
files with official reversed artwork when the brand owners supply it.
